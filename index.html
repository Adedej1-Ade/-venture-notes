I write about the startups and the shifts worth paying attention to.

I’m Victor Ade-Oduntan — everyone calls me Ade. I am an investment professional who, over the past four years, has sat on both sides of venture investing and debt financing, supporting venture investors while also working as a finance operator within high-growth startups and a lending institution.

This path started with a personal experience that pushed me toward finance as a way to help businesses grow. Working within debt finance, I saw its limits firsthand, particularly how much promising businesses still struggled to access the funding they needed, and looking for a solution that addressed that is what led me to venture capital.

Today, I focus on identifying promising startups and the trends that the right founders can ride to build something that matters. My interests sit mainly in B2B businesses across healthtech, climate, and fintech, with a particular interest in founders and businesses working to expand economic opportunity in underserved communities.
